3. YouTube videos (only after you click)

Exhibitions can have an intro video hosted on YouTube, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. No connection to YouTube or Google is made until you click “Play video”. Before that, only a notice is shown. When you click, your browser loads the YouTube player and transmits your IP address and information about your device to Google. YouTube may store information in your browser (local storage) and, if you are logged in to a Google account, assign your viewing to that account. We use the privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which limits tracking by YouTube, but we have no control over what Google does with the data.

Legal basis: your consent, Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and {{{consentLaw}}}. Google LLC in the USA may receive data; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission considers an adequate level of protection (Art. 45 GDPR). Details: policies.google.com/privacy.

Your consent is remembered in your browser so that you do not have to confirm it for every video. You can withdraw it at any time: . After that, the notice is shown again before any video.

4. Local storage in your browser

The website stores the following information on your device, all strictly necessary for the function you requested and therefore without consent ({{{storageLaw}}}): the exhibition data you just opened, for a few minutes, so the exhibition appears instantly after the intro video (session storage, deleted when you close the tab); your YouTube decision from section 3; and, only if you use the shop, the contents of your cart and the country of delivery you chose (local storage, until you empty the cart or order). No identifiers are created. The cart is only sent to us when you open the cart page or go to the payment.

5. Contact by e-mail

If you write to us, we process your e-mail address and the content of your message to answer you. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR when the request concerns a contract, otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Messages are deleted once the matter is settled, unless statutory retention periods apply.

6. Orders and payment (shop)

When you order prints, we process the items, your name, e-mail address, shipping and billing address and the payment status to conclude and fulfil the contract, to ship the prints and to send you the order confirmation and the shipping notice. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (contract) and Art. 6 (1) (c) GDPR (statutory retention). Orders are kept for the statutory retention period for business records ({{{retentionNote}}}), then deleted.

Payment is handled by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., 1 Grand Canal Street Lower, Grand Canal Dock, Dublin, D02 H210, Ireland. When you click “Continue to payment” you are taken to a payment page of Stripe, where you enter your address and payment details directly with Stripe. We never see your card or bank details; we only receive the result of the payment, your name, e-mail address and addresses. Stripe also processes data to prevent fraud and is responsible for this itself. Stripe may transfer data to Stripe, Inc. in the USA, which is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR). Details: stripe.com/privacy.

If you ask for the price of a print, we store your name, e-mail address and message to answer you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR, request before a contract) and delete them once the request is settled. If you withdraw through the withdrawal function, we store your statement with the order and send you an acknowledgement of receipt.

7. E-mails

Order confirmations, shipping notices and answers are sent through the mail server of {{{mailProvider}}}, which processes the e-mails on our behalf (Art. 28 GDPR).

8. Administration area

The content of this website is managed in a password-protected area. Only editors log in there; a login cookie is set for them alone. Visitors never receive this cookie.

For the accounts of this area we store, as proof, when they accepted the terms for contributors (date, version and a copy of the text) and, for every upload, who confirmed the rights to the file and when. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (the terms of use of the account) and Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in being able to prove who is responsible for a file). The data is kept as long as the account or the file exists and as long as claims are possible.

9. Your rights

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR), to rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. To exercise these rights, contact the controller named in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your residence or workplace. {{{authorityNote}}}
